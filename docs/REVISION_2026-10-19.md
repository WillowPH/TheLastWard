# Chapter 1 correction pass — 19 October 2026

Build: `last-ward-ch1-survival-review-5`. Start a new game; earlier build saves
remain in separate browser-storage namespaces. The project retains separate code,
data, artwork, audio and launcher files. Chapter 2 remains outside this build.

## Survival and death

- A fatal outcome latches the run as dead before the death animation/dialog ends.
  Healing, crafting, recharging, delayed inputs, combat, travel and timers cannot
  revive it or consume supplies. Explicit checkpoint/save loading starts a living
  state again. Death-screen checkpoint buttons accept ordinary mouse clicks.
- The sprinkler incident causes immediate difficulty-scaled injury. Wet clothing
  persists until deliberate warming; walking or waiting for a turn counter no
  longer dries it. Leaving the cold or entering warmth stops local exposure.
- The purge handle opens three exterior sashes once. Health and composure fall
  while they remain open. All three individual catches must be closed; the linked
  linen-return compartment then provides a finite optional supply reward.
- Powered warm shelters expose a Dry off and warm up action. The lit laundry stove
  is an alternative. Local exposure is recoverable rather than a permanent meter.
- Saved story trauma can earn a limited number of body visions and imaginary
  encounters, even after partial composure recovery. False combat spends actual
  ammunition, cannot kill the player, expires and cannot replay indefinitely.
- Existing ammunition caches have larger finite quantities. World configuration
  never refills collected caches. Normal Subject X evasion buys twelve game turns
  of recovery; duplicate arrival checks no longer consume that interval twice.
- Infected and Subject X retreat paths run arrival incidents once, using the
  original movement cost. The elevator still requires earned Chapter 1 progress.

## Light, records and interface

- The flashlight is physically taken from the abandoned coat at reception.
- Room art, examination and battery rules use the same local lighting test.
  Generator power does not falsely brighten an unpowered local circuit. The light
  stays off in sufficiently lit rooms. A failed dark search displays “It’s too dark
  to see” in the scene and puts practical guidance in Thoughts.
- Reading pauses action and real-time battery/exposure costs. Native window and
  shutter state artwork receives the same lighting as its room.
- A fixture with several findings shows a selector and one card at a time.
  Camera/recording and journal/winter-note contents no longer stack under icons.
  Closing the first finding does not require reopening the cabinet for its next item.
- Damaged handwritten and typed documents paginate in a bounded reading surface,
  including on narrow screens. Full text remains available, with a transcription
  toggle. Thoughts show a concise card and can open their full entry in the Journal.
- Files have subject labels, location labels, search and Current leads. Puzzle
  dialogs link only relevant records already collected; they do not autofill answers.
- Items can be combined directly from the right inventory. Recipes, lighter fuel,
  weapon/ammunition pairs and stabilizer upgrades use the same controls. Incompatible
  pairs consume nothing. First acquiring wire explains its reinforced-battery use.
- Confirmed disposal of a spent/unneeded item returns to the room. Unfinished shared
  keys and required tools remain protected. Supplies and photographs retain icons.
- Terminology is Terminal, Floppy Disk and Storage box. Legacy Ink Ribbon item
  names normalize to Floppy Disk. Existing painted containers provide three extra
  shared Storage boxes in Pharmacy, Staff Lounge and Records.
- Empty, cooling or otherwise unavailable chargers have no misleading action target.
