# Four-room visual review

Open `downloads/The_Last_Ward_Chapter_1.html` from the extracted branch ZIP.
Review Lobby → Security → East Corridor → Patient Wing before expanding the new
reverse-view art standard to the remaining rooms. All 27 rooms remain playable.

## What to check

1. Clara has adult height and meets the floor beside the desk.
2. Doorways have physical door art, without black rectangular patches.
3. Examine opens a panel by the selected object. Take/Leave is explicit.
4. Turn around shows a complete room view in the four review rooms.
5. Rain, CRT and fluorescent/lamp effects remain subtle; Scene Animation can be disabled.
6. The enlarged map separates Isolation’s spur from Room 12 → Children’s Ward.
7. Peeking shows the room through cover-specific openings.
8. The negative remains when left, disappears when taken, and stays collected.
9. Read reports move to Files without leaving a repeat inspection hotspot.
10. An empty firearm explains why the patient choice cannot proceed; a successful
    choice shows a persistent covered bed.

## Boundaries

Full reverse artwork is provided for the four review rooms. The remaining rooms
have return controls and their original distinct compositions; the generic isolated
door cutout has been removed. Further per-room removable-prop conversion remains.
The negative is the explicit clean-background/removable-object example in this build.
Cover frames are rendered SVG geometry with real openings over the actual room;
they are not full photographic locker-interior artwork. Animation is layered, not video.
