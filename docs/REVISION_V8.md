# Chapter 1 balance and progression — v8

Build `last-ward-ch1-chapter-review-8`, 20 October 2026.

## Encounter and supply balance

Normal uses nine finite authored infected instead of three. Story uses five and
Nightmare eleven, with the existing once-only window breach kept separate. Noise
alerts those enemies; it does not manufacture replacements. Cleared enemies stay
cleared. Continued older runs gain new encounters only in unvisited rooms.

A fresh infected survives two Utility Knife cuts. Close-range finishing remains
useful after weakening it; forcing a gap through a fresh Normal enemy costs 14 HP
and leaves it alive for a return visit. Deliberate firearm aim trades time for
accuracy and interruption. Normal's finite ammunition stores include 60 standard
handgun rounds and 12 more in the optional purge compartment, with difficulty
adjustments and no repeat-pickup farming. A single finite first-aid kit in the
rear staff locker supports the longer mid-chapter encounter route. Supply locations now have clearer names,
etched interaction brackets and contextual preparation thoughts.

The final route requires a real opening past Subject X: a firearm knockdown, a
trap that actually caught it, or successful cover evasion. Retreat counters and an
empty, wasted trap cannot meet this requirement. If the player has avoided every
meaningful encounter, Subject X breaches the generator shelter near the end.
Knockdown requires two, three or four handgun hits on Story, Normal or Nightmare
respectively; heavy shells require one, two or two. Subject X counterattacks between
uninterrupted impacts and later recovers.
The shotgun remains optional and now exists in an authorized rear theatre drawer;
a once-only thought before descent points to it if it was left behind.

## Reasoning and progression

The existing hospital layout remains fixed. More work happens in its rear views:
critical transfer records, the encrypted drive, fuses, patrol ammunition and the
optional shotgun have authored furniture locations there.

- The service crank operates a required two-stage lift-pit pump. Its collected
  instructions explain priming and drainage; incorrect settings preserve the tool.
- A ten-amp transfer rack requires the appropriate safeguards and isolation of
  unrelated circuits. Its load plates and collected records support the deduction.
- The final fuse carrier has numbered contacts rather than matching-name sockets.
  Relationships on the collected transfer carbon determine a unique arrangement.
  Wrong trials consume no plugs and can be corrected.
- Starting the generator, draining the pit, isolating the supply and testing the
  interlock produce explicit results. Only the complete shared progression gate
  reports the elevator unlocked.
- Security's corridor leaf is manually released from inside Security, creating an
  outage-safe shortcut. It no longer asks for a redundant access key.
- The late chapel photograph adds story evidence rather than repeating a solved
  lock solution.

Files separates Progression objectives from Story records and All records. Objective
cards link the clues actually collected, show short excerpts and retain completed
records. Puzzle readers return to the active mechanism without inserting answers.
A fully spent lighter and its unused fluid are automatically removed on Normal and
Story after both permanent ignition points are lit; Nightmare keeps manual discard.

## Horror, sound and visual corrections

Earned trauma is checked again during quiet exploration after story dialogues close.
It can produce two visual episodes and one imaginary fight per run, separated by
saved cooldowns. The visions use the current facing. Imaginary combat can spend
real ammunition but cannot kill or injure the player. Story incidents themselves
remain once-only.

The sprinkler shock and continued exposure now cause meaningful injury; wet travel
continues costing health until deliberate drying/warming. Open purge windows and
cold rooms are local hazards with warnings and recovery routes. Reading and Pause
protect the player's time to understand clues and preserve encounter deadlines.

The active breathing beds are replaced with restrained original heartbeat cues.
Healthy exploration uses room ambience, with brief pulse cues for an immediate
threat; caution and danger have distinct paced pulses. Reading quiets the pulse.
No repeated pain-grunt or synthetic air-breath loop is selected.

Real enemy textures are normalized into cached opaque-body images while preserving
transparent surroundings and native proportions. Pixel tests compare contrasting
backgrounds. PEEK has usable physical apertures, local eye adaptation and live
resize alignment. The rest of the hiding shell stays black. Kessler's revised
standing and slumped artwork has attached hands and a coherent aftermath; his
completed hotspot retires.

Narration and Thoughts have no opaque backing fill. Desktop captions sit over the
whole native painting, with fine borders and text contrast. Long thoughts reserve
space for their Journal control rather than clipping. Mouse turning, nav-only
Pause and the separate multi-file project remain. Scene refreshes wait until a
held mouse gesture ends, so a scheduled redraw cannot cancel a turn.

## Continued saves

Recognized v5, v6 and v7 saves remain loadable. Cleared enemies, collected resources
and used object IDs are retained; previously earned late transfer clearance is
preserved. Finale and glass checkpoint migration run before stamping the new build ID.
A new run gives the complete encounter and puzzle progression from its beginning.
